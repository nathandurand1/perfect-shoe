About these numbers

Landing force (3.0 body weights), sideways force (1.6 body weights) and the energy-return figures for the Nike, adidas and typical shoe come from published studies and lab tests (J Sports Sci Med 2011; Lam 2017; RunRepeat). This shoe's 76% return, the split between parts, the ankle-force share and the safety scores are design targets, not measurements from a built shoe.
