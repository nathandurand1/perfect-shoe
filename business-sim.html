How the volume was chosen

How price changes sales. Sales are scaled by (price / $160)-2, a price elasticity of -2. Published studies of brand-level price response average -1.76 (Tellis, 367 estimates) and -2.62 (Bijmolt et al., 1,851 estimates); I used -2 because a big brand like Nike is a bit less price-sensitive than the average brand. A price cut can add at most 50% more pairs, because factories, stores and the market can only absorb so much. The -2 is an estimate, not a measured number for this shoe.

The Excel model that matches this page has every input, the sources and a sensitivity grid. Zoom unit, carbon plate and Filon costs have no public price, so they are my estimates; the sourced numbers are market size, Nike's share, channel mix, tooling costs and the tariff rate. Figures are pre-tax and leave out inventory financing, royalties and athlete contracts.
